Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to book and manage visits on this website. It aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

Known issues

  • The "Chat with us" badge comes from an external provider. Its text contrast is too low (about 3 to 1 instead of 4.5 to 1). The provider has promised a fix; until then, call us on 0800 000 000 instead.

How we test

Every change is checked automatically with axe-core against WCAG 2.2 AA, with keyboard-only tests, and with screenshots compared against approved versions. We also test with a screen reader before each release.
